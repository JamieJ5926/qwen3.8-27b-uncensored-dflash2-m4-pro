I got Qwen3.8-27B uncensored running about twice as fast on my 48GB M4 Pro
MacBook Pro — 12.35 -> 25.48 tok/s — just by pairing incoai's stock DFlash2
draft model with orcarouter's separately abliterated MLX build. No retraining,
no custom draft: the stock draft slots straight onto the modified target and
mean acceptance length is 4.87 out of a 5-token block.

Setup: 4-bit MLX, greedy decoding, 20 HumanEval prompts, block size 5, paired
baseline vs DFlash under identical settings. Model and caches on an external
HDD, everything else stopped.

It's a benchmark dataset and recipe, not a model or redistributed weights —
numbers are one config on one machine.
